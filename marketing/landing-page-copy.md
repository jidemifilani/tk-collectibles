## Features grid (6 cards)

### 1. Shop that matches your socials  
Curated catalog, category filters, “New Drops,” and product pages with size & condition — just like you describe items in your posts.

### 2. Sale pricing that sells  
Show the **original price** crossed out and your **discount price** clear. Customers see the deal instantly.

### 3. Paystack checkout  
Accept card, bank transfer, and USSD. Orders are verified automatically — less “I’ve paid” confusion.

### 4. Contact & customer care  
Dedicated contact page with a **tap-to-call** support number, WhatsApp, Instagram, email, and hours.

### 5. Admin panel — no developer needed  
Add wears, upload photos from your device, mark items sold out, and edit your homepage text anytime.

### 6. Your brand, your colors  
Upload your logo (auto-resizes to fit). Pick 7 theme colors so the whole site feels like your page.

## How it works (3 steps)

**Step 1 — Set up your store**  
Add your logo, colors, customer care number, and social links in admin.

**Step 2 — List your drop**  
Upload product photos from your phone. Set actual price + discount price. Mark featured items for “New Drops.”

**Step 3 — Share one link**  
Put the store link in your bio. Customers browse, cart, and pay. You fulfill and track orders in admin.

## FAQ

**Do I need a website already?**  
No. This is your store — one link for your bio.

**Can I update products myself?**  
Yes. Upload images, prices, and stock from the admin panel on your phone.

**What payment methods do customers use?**  
Paystack: debit/credit card, bank transfer, and USSD (Nigeria).

**Will it work on mobile?**  
Yes. The store is built mobile-first for social traffic.

**Can I use my own logo and colors?**  
Yes. Upload your logo and choose your brand palette in settings.

**What about delivery?**  
Customers enter their address at checkout. You manage shipping via your usual process (courier, pickup, etc.).
