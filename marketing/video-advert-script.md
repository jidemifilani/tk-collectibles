## 30-SECOND VERSION — Shot-by-shot storyboard

### Scene 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:03)
- **Visual:** Phone screen recording your IG DMs full of “How much?” messages.
- **On-screen text:** “Still selling in the DMs? 😩”
- **Voiceover (VO):** “Tired of answering ‘how much’ all day?”
- **Audio:** Beat drops in.

### Scene 2 — PROBLEM (0:03–0:07)
- **Visual:** Quick montage — screenshots, lost messages, a confused “did you pay?” chat.
- **On-screen text:** “Lost messages. Missed sales. No proof of payment.”
- **VO:** “Your drops deserve more than a chat thread.”

### Scene 3 — REVEAL (0:07–0:12)
- **Visual:** Screen recording — open the store link, homepage loads with logo + hero banner. Smooth scroll through the shop grid.
- **On-screen text:** “Meet your new online store 🛍️”
- **VO:** “Introducing [Store Name] — your thrift shop, one link.”

### Scene 4 — FEATURE: PRICING (0:12–0:16)
- **Visual:** Zoom on a product card showing ~~₦[old]~~ → **₦[sale]** with the “-30%” badge.
- **On-screen text:** “Real prices. Clear discounts.”
- **VO:** “Customers see the deal instantly — no more DM-for-price.”

### Scene 5 — FEATURE: CHECKOUT (0:16–0:21)
- **Visual:** Screen recording — tap “Add to cart” → checkout → Paystack screen (card / transfer / USSD).
- **On-screen text:** “Secure checkout with Paystack 🔒”
- **VO:** “Pay with card, transfer, or USSD. Done in seconds.”

### Scene 6 — FEATURE: CONTROL (0:21–0:25)
- **Visual:** Admin panel — upload a photo from phone, set price, item appears live.
- **On-screen text:** “Upload from your phone. No developer needed.”
- **VO:** “Add new wears in seconds — anytime.”

### Scene 7 — CTA (0:25–0:30)
- **Visual:** Store homepage with logo. Big animated button. End card with link + WhatsApp.
- **On-screen text:** “Shop now 👉 [link]  ·  WhatsApp [phone]”
- **VO:** “Stop chasing payments. Start selling properly. Link in bio.”
- **Audio:** Music swells, soft outro.

## How to film the screen recordings

1. Open your live store on your phone.
2. Use the built-in screen recorder.
3. Record these clips (5–8s each):
   - Homepage load + scroll
   - Shop grid scroll
   - One product page (show the crossed-out price + discount)
   - Add to cart → checkout → Paystack page (use TEST mode)
   - Admin: upload a product photo + it going live
4. Import clips into CapCut, trim to the storyboard timings, add captions + music.

> Tip: Speed up screen recordings 1.5×–2× so it feels snappy.
